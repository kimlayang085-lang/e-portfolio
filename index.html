About

I'm currently pursuing two degrees at once: a B.Sc. in Software Engineering at Limkokwing University and a Bachelor of Professional Communication at IFL. It's an unusual combination, but it means I build things and I know how to explain them whether that's writing documentation, presenting to a client, or just making sure a project's purpose is clear to people outside the room.

My coursework spans two very different worlds: software architecture and systems design on one side, and communication theory, writing, and media on the other. I spend as much time debugging code as I do drafting reports and presentations.

I'm currently looking for internships or part-time roles where both skill sets are useful software development, technical writing, or product/communications roles at a tech company.
